# HDR-003 · Pages cannot be framed by other sites (clickjacking protection)

## Why it matters
If another site can place your page in an invisible frame, it can trick visitors into clicking buttons they cannot see. The `frame-ancestors` directive controls who may frame the page.

## How to test
```bash
curl -sI https://example.com/ | grep -iE 'content-security-policy|x-frame-options'
```

## Pass criteria
`frame-ancestors 'none'` or `'self'` (or an explicit allow-list) is present in the CSP. `X-Frame-Options: DENY` or `SAMEORIGIN` is sent as a fallback for very old browsers.

## Fix
Add `frame-ancestors 'self'` to the CSP and `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN`. If the site is meant to be embedded, list the exact embedding origins instead.

## Risk and rollback
Setting `'none'` breaks legitimate embedding (for example inside your own admin or a partner's portal). Check for known embedders first.
