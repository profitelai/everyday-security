# HDR-005 · Referrer-Policy limits what URLs leak to other sites

## Why it matters
The browser sends the previous page's address to the next site. URLs often contain tokens, search terms or account identifiers that should not leave your origin.

## How to test
```bash
curl -sI https://example.com/ | grep -i referrer-policy
```

## Pass criteria
A policy is present and is `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`, `same-origin`, or `no-referrer`. `unsafe-url` and `no-referrer-when-downgrade` fail.

## Fix
`Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin` as the general choice; `no-referrer` for sensitive areas such as account and billing pages.

## Risk and rollback
Some analytics and affiliate tools rely on full referrers; check reports after the change.
