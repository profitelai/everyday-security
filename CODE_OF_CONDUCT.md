# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a respectful, useful place for people who care about security.

## Expected
- Be courteous. Assume good faith. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give evidence for security claims and say when you are unsure.
- Respect privacy: never post other people's data, credentials or scan results.
- Work only on systems you own or have permission to test.

## Not acceptable
- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sharing exploits or scan results against systems you do not have permission to test.
- Pressuring maintainers to disclose or fix issues on your schedule.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions, and block contributors who break these rules. Report a concern privately through the contact details at https://www.profitell.com/.
